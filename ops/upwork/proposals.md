# Upwork proposal templates

**Workflow:** Jose pastes a job post (or its link text) into chat → I fill the
matching template with specifics from that posting → he pastes it in. ~2 min of
his time per proposal.

## Rules that override everything below

1. **Only the first ~2 lines are visible** in the client's proposal list. They
   decide whether the rest is read. Never open with "Hi, I'm Jose" or "I saw
   your posting" — open with something only someone who read the job could say.
2. **Name something specific from their posting or their store.** One concrete
   detail beats three paragraphs of capability claims.
3. **Never claim a client, a result, a testimonial or a years-of-experience
   figure we don't have.** Revenue is $0.00 and there are no past clients. The
   samples are real; that is the entire proof base.
4. **One ask.** A proposal with two questions gets neither answered.
5. **Under 150 words.** Clients read dozens.
6. **Do the work first where the job allows it.** A rewritten line inside the
   proposal outperforms any description of ability — and unlike cold email, this
   buyer is already shopping.
7. **Check the Connects cost before applying** (6 for most, 10–16 for
   high-budget). 80 free at signup + 10/month. Roughly 13 free proposals, so
   skip any job we cannot prove we can do.

## Job-screening checklist — skip rather than spend Connects

Skip if **any** of these are true:
- Posting requires years of experience, an agency, or a named client list.
- It demands **human-written only / no AI** copy. We do not misrepresent how
  the work is produced, and Loya Media's production is AI-assisted. Skip it.
  (ContentWriters, Verblio, Draft and others enforce this with detection tools.)
- Payment method unverified AND budget under $100.
- Niche we cannot prove — technical, medical, legal, crypto, academic.
- 50+ proposals already and the budget is under $150.

Prioritise: food/drink/craft/pet brands · small catalogs · Shopify stores ·
anything where the posting mentions a founder story or rebrand · clients with
verified payment and a hire history.
