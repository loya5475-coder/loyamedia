## The read

Loya Media is not a startup problem. It is a **distribution problem**.

What already exists:
- A live, well-built site at loyamedia.com with a real offer
- A priced product ladder from $150 to $2,000/mo
- A zero-risk lead magnet (3 free rewrites)
- A business Gmail, Google Analytics, Apollo.io and Hunter.io accounts

What does not exist:
- **A single inbound lead.** 30 days of inbox history: 18 threads, all newsletters and tool notifications. Zero prospects. Zero clients. Zero dollars.

The site is a storefront on a street with no foot traffic. Nothing about the
site needs fixing before money can arrive. What has to happen is that a few
hundred of the right people need to find out it exists — and the fastest way
to make a stranger care is to hand them finished work before asking for
anything.

## The strategy: Proof-First Outbound

Standard cold email asks for attention and offers a promise.
We invert it: **do the work first, deliver it free, then name a price.**

Every email we send contains a real rewrite of that specific business's real
copy — written before contact, attached with no strings. The ask is not "can we
talk." The ask is "the rest of your catalog is $800."

This works for three reasons:
1. It cannot be mistaken for a template. It is visibly about them.
2. It reverses risk completely — they already have the value before deciding.
3. It demonstrates the product instead of describing it. The email *is* the sample.

It also happens to be the exact thing an AI operator can do at a volume a
human agency cannot: research a brand, write its copy, personalize the pitch,
and send — end to end, dozens per day, without a copy-paste step.

## The ladder (close small, expand later)

Cold prospects do not sign $1,000/mo retainers. They buy a $150 thing, like it,
and then buy the $800 thing. Lead with the small yes.

| Offer | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Blog post (1,000–1,500 w) | $150 | Easiest yes. First-dollar offer. |
| 30-day social caption pack | $350 | Low-friction volume buy. |
| 5-email welcome sequence | $400 | Highest perceived ROI per dollar. |
| Homepage + About copy | $500 | For brands mid-rebrand. |
| Full store rewrite (30 products) | $800 | **Primary cold-email ask.** |
| Starter / Growth / Authority retainer | $500 / $1,000 / $2,000 mo | Post-delivery upsell only. |

Founding-client 50% discount is a **closing lever held in reserve**, not a
headline. Discounting before there is an objection halves revenue for nothing.

## Lanes

**Lane 1 — Cold outbound (volume).** Researched brands, completed rewrites,
sent from the business Gmail. ~10–15/day, ramping. This is the engine.

**Lane 2 — Warm and local (conversion).** People Jose already knows, and
businesses in his city. Converts several times better than cold and needs no
prospect list. This is where the first dollar most likely comes from.

**Lane 3 — Delivered-client expansion.** Every completed project ends with a
retainer offer and a referral ask. Cheapest revenue in the whole operation.

## What only Jose can do

Three things. Each is a one-time setup, and each is a hard blocker on money
actually arriving.

1. **A payment link** (Stripe Payment Links or PayPal invoicing). Without this
   there is no mechanism for a dollar to reach you. ~10 minutes.
2. **A prospect export** from Apollo.io or Hunter.io — you already pay for
   both. 200–500 e-commerce founders/owners with verified emails, as CSV,
   dropped in `ops/prospects/`. This is the single highest-leverage 10 minutes
   available; it is the difference between 15 emails a day and 3.
3. **A physical mailing address** for the email footer. Commercial email
   legally requires one (CAN-SPAM). Home address, PO box, or a virtual
   mailbox — any is fine, but it has to be real.

Nice-to-have, not blocking: a `josel@loyamedia.com` send-as alias on the Gmail
account. Sending from a domain that matches the site materially improves both
credibility and inbox placement.

## Guardrails (these protect the money, they are not hedges)

- **Volume discipline.** A personal Gmail blasting 100 cold emails a day gets
  rate-limited or suspended — and the account *is* the revenue channel. We ramp:
  ~10/day week 1, ~15/day week 2+. Slow is faster than dead.
- **Real opt-out, honored immediately.** Every email carries one. Anyone who
  asks goes on the permanent do-not-contact list, no follow-up, ever.
- **No fabricated proof.** No invented testimonials, no fake client logos, no
  claimed results that didn't happen. It's fraud, and it's also the fastest way
  to lose the one thing this business is selling: trust.
- **Every claim is checkable.** The scoreboard reports what happened, including
  the weeks where nothing did.
