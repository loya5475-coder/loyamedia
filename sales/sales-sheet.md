# Loya Media — Cold Call Sales Sheet
### Selling websites to blue-collar businesses (roofers, framers, concrete, etc.)

**The one rule:** On the first call you are NOT selling a website. You are selling a **10-minute meeting**. The website sells itself at the meeting — because you'll already have it built.

---

## Before you dial (2 minutes per lead)

1. Google the business name + city. Confirm they still have no website (the spreadsheet may be a few days old).
2. Read their Google reviews. Grab ONE specific detail — "saw you did that metal roof job on the east side," "saw Maria's review about your stucco work." This one detail is what makes you sound local instead of like a telemarketer.
3. Note their rating and review count. A 4.8★ business with 30 reviews and no website is your dream client — say that to them.

---

## The opening script (first 20 seconds decide everything)

> "Hey, is this [Name / the owner of ___]? My name's Jose, I'm here local in Santa Teresa — I'm not selling insurance or anything, I'll be real quick.
>
> I was looking up [trade] companies in [city] and I found you guys — you've got a [4.8] rating on Google, people clearly love your work. But when I clicked around, **you don't have a website**, so all those people searching '[trade] near me' are finding your competitors instead of you.
>
> That's literally what I do — I build websites for [trade] companies around here. Can I ask you one quick question?"

*(Wait for yes.)*

> "When someone finds you on Google right now, where do they end up? Just the phone number?"

Let them talk. Whatever they say, you respond:

> "Right — so here's my offer. **Give me 10 minutes this week, in person or over the phone, and I'll show you a website I built for your company — already done, with your name on it, your reviews, your services.** If you hate it, you never hear from me again. If you like it, we talk. Does Tuesday or Thursday work better?"

**Always offer two times, never "when are you free?"** ("Tuesday afternoon or Thursday morning?")

> 🇲🇽 **Border-region note:** A lot of these owners are more comfortable in Spanish. If they answer in Spanish, switch. The same script works: *"Le construí una página web a su compañía — ya está hecha, con su nombre y sus reseñas. Déme 10 minutos y se la enseño."* Being bilingual is a real competitive advantage here — use it.

---

## Discovery questions (if they engage on the first call)

- "How do most of your customers find you right now?"
- "When someone gets 3 quotes, what do you think they check before deciding who to call back?" *(Answer: Google. And two of the three have websites.)*
- "You ever lose a job to a company that does worse work than you?" *(They ALL say yes. That's your moment: "That company probably just looks better online. That's fixable in a week.")*
- "Do you have pictures of your work on your phone?" *(They always do — that's your gallery content, and it gets them picturing the site.)*

---

## Objection handling

**"I get all my work from word of mouth."**
> "And that's exactly why this works — word of mouth means people are already looking you up by name. When they Google you and find nothing, some of them quietly call someone else. The website isn't to replace word of mouth, it's to catch the people word of mouth already sent you."

**"How much is it?" (on the first call)**
> "Depends on what you need, but I'll tell you it's a few hundred bucks one time, not some monthly contract for thousands. Honestly, let me just show you the site first — takes 10 minutes and the price will make sense when you're looking at it. Tuesday or Thursday?"

*(Don't get pinned to a number before they've seen the demo. The demo justifies the price.)*

**"I have a Facebook page."**
> "Facebook's good — keep it. But when someone Googles 'roofer in Las Cruces,' Facebook pages barely show up, and half the people who click get hit with a login wall. A real website shows up on Google, works for everybody, and links right back to your Facebook."

**"My nephew / my cousin can build one."**
> "Maybe he can! But it's been how long and it's not up yet? I'll have yours live in a week, and when something breaks at 7am I'm the one who answers, not family at Thanksgiving."

**"Too expensive / no budget right now."**
> "I hear you. One question — what's an average job worth to you? [They say $5,000–$15,000.] The site costs less than one job, one time. If it brings you ONE customer a year it paid for itself. And it'll bring more than one."

**"Call me later / I'm on a roof right now."**
> "Say less — what time do you get down, like 5? I'll call you at 5:30 for two minutes." *(Then actually call at 5:30. Being exact builds more trust than anything you say.)*

**"Not interested." (hard no)**
> "No problem, I appreciate the time. If you ever want to see what your company would look like online, my number's [number] — I'm local, not a call center. Have a good one." *(Leave the door open, mark the sheet, move on. Never argue.)*

## Voicemail script (you'll hit voicemail 60%+ of the time)

> "Hey [Name], this is Jose, I'm local in Santa Teresa. I built a sample website for [Business Name] — it's got your reviews and your services on it and it looks sharp. I want to show it to you, takes 10 minutes, no cost to look. Call or text me at [number]. Again, Jose, [number]. Thanks."

**Then send a text:** *"Hey [Name], Jose here — just left you a voicemail. I build websites for [trade] companies around [city] and made a sample one for you. Can I text you the link?"* — texting the actual demo link is your highest-converting move. The site does the selling.

## Follow-up cadence (per lead)

| Touch | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1 | Call → voicemail + text |
| 2 | Day 3 | Call at a different time of day |
| 3 | Day 5 | Text the demo link with a screenshot |
| 4 | Day 10 | Last call: "closing out my list for [city], wanted to give you last shot at the site I built" |
| — | Day 30+ | Recycle into next month's list |

Most sales happen on touches 3–5. Most callers quit after touch 1. That gap is the whole business.

## The appointment (demo → close)

**Bring:** laptop or tablet with the demo site loaded (and a phone hotspot — don't depend on their wifi). Have it open on THEIR phone too — trades owners live on their phones and seeing their business look that good *on their own phone* is the closing moment.

**The flow:**
1. **Zero small talk beyond 2 minutes.** "Let me just show you." Hand them the phone with the site up.
2. **Shut up and let them scroll.** Do not narrate every section. They're seeing their name, their reviews, their trade looking like a million bucks. Silence is your closer.
3. Point out 3 things max: click-to-call button ("every visitor is one tap from calling you"), their real reviews ("that's Maria's review, it's already selling for you"), the quote form ("jobs come in while you sleep").
4. **The price reveal:**
> "So here's the deal. Sites like this from an agency run $2,500 to $5,000 — that's not me hustling you, Google it. I'm building my portfolio here in [city], so I do it for **$800 one-time**. That covers the whole build, your own domain — [BusinessName].com — and getting it live on Google. Then it's **$50 a month** for hosting, security, updates, and me being one text away when you want something changed. No contract — if you ever want out, it's your domain, you keep it."
5. **Then be quiet.** First person to talk loses.
6. If yes → collect the deposit **on the spot** (PayPal invoice from your phone — see delivery playbook). A "yes" without money is a "maybe."
7. If "let me think about it" → "Totally fair. What's the part you want to think over — the money or whether it'll actually bring you work?" *(Smoke out the real objection and handle it. Then:)* "Tell you what — I'll hold this exact site for you until Friday. After that I offer the [trade]-in-[city] spot to the next company on my list." *(True scarcity: you ARE calling their competitors.)*

---

## Daily numbers to expect (be realistic)

- 20 dials/day → ~8 conversations → ~2 interested → ~1 booked appointment
- 5 appointments → ~2 closes (demo-first closes 30–50%; describing-only closes ~10%)
- **20 dials a day, 5 days a week ≈ 4–8 new clients/month.** The math works — the game is dials and follow-ups, not perfect scripts.

Track every call in the spreadsheet (Call Status column) the second you hang up. Your memory will lie to you by Friday.
