# landingsite.ai Master Prompt Template
### Paste-and-fill prompt for building luxury, high-converting trade-company sites

**How to use:**
1. Open the business's Google Business Profile (Google Maps listing). Copy: their services, hours, service area, 3–5 of their best reviews (with first names), and note what photos they have.
2. Fill in every `[BRACKET]` below. Delete any line that doesn't apply.
3. Paste the whole thing into landingsite.ai.
4. After it generates, use follow-up prompts (bottom of this doc) to polish.

---

## THE MASTER PROMPT

```
Build a premium, high-converting website for a local [TRADE — e.g., roofing] company.

BUSINESS INFO
- Business name: [BUSINESS NAME]
- Location: [CITY, STATE]
- Service area: [e.g., El Paso, Santa Teresa, Sunland Park, Las Cruces and surrounding areas]
- Phone: [PHONE — this is the #1 conversion goal of the site]
- Years in business: [X years / family-owned / licensed & insured — whatever is true]
- Services: [LIST 4–8 SERVICES, e.g., shingle roof replacement, metal roofing, roof repair, storm damage inspection, gutters]
- Google rating: [4.8] stars with [32] reviews

DESIGN DIRECTION
Luxurious and high-end but masculine and trustworthy — think premium contractor, not tech startup. Dark, rich color palette: [deep charcoal/navy] with [gold/amber/orange] accents. Big bold headlines, high-contrast sections, generous spacing. Full-width hero with a dramatic background image of [TRADE] work. This should feel like the most established, professional [TRADE] company in [CITY] — a company you'd trust with a $15,000 job. Mobile-first: most visitors will be on phones.

PAGE STRUCTURE (single scrolling page + these sections)
1. HERO: Headline like "[CITY]'s Trusted [TRADE] Experts" with subheadline mentioning [X years / rating]. Two buttons: "Call Now: [PHONE]" (click-to-call, high-contrast color) and "Get a Free Quote". Show the star rating under the buttons.
2. TRUST BAR: badges — Licensed & Insured, [X] Years Experience, Free Estimates, [4.8]★ on Google, Se Habla Español.
3. SERVICES: card grid of the services listed above, each with a short benefit-focused description and a "Get a Quote" link.
4. WHY CHOOSE US: 3–4 points — honest local pricing, owner on every job, fast response, clean job sites. Written in plain confident language, no corporate buzzwords.
5. REVIEWS: testimonial section using these REAL Google reviews:
   - "[REVIEW QUOTE 1]" — [First name]
   - "[REVIEW QUOTE 2]" — [First name]
   - "[REVIEW QUOTE 3]" — [First name]
   Include the overall [4.8]★ [32] reviews badge linking to their Google listing.
6. GALLERY / OUR WORK: grid placeholder for 6–8 job photos (before/after style if possible).
7. SERVICE AREA: list the towns served: [LIST TOWNS]. Small map or styled list.
8. FINAL CTA: full-width section — "Ready to get started? Call [PHONE] today for a free estimate." Giant click-to-call button.
9. QUOTE FORM: name, phone, service needed (dropdown of their services), short message. Keep it 4 fields max.
10. FOOTER: business name, phone, service area, hours [HOURS], link to their Facebook page [URL if they have one].

CONVERSION RULES
- The phone number appears in the header (sticky on mobile), the hero, mid-page, and the final CTA — always click-to-call.
- A sticky "Call Now" bar on mobile at all times.
- Every section has exactly one job: get a call or a quote form fill. No blog, no filler pages.
- Write all copy at an 8th-grade reading level, confident and local. No stock corporate phrases like "we strive for excellence."
[- IF BILINGUAL AREA: Add a short "Se habla español" mention in the hero and trust bar.]

SEO BASICS
- Title tag: "[BUSINESS NAME] | [TRADE] in [CITY], [STATE]"
- H1 contains "[TRADE] in [CITY]"
- Mention the service-area towns naturally in the copy so the site can rank for "[trade] + [each town]".
```

## Follow-up prompts to polish the generated site

- "Make the hero headline bigger and more dramatic, and darken the background image overlay so the text pops."
- "The call-to-action buttons should be [amber/gold] and larger on mobile."
- "Rewrite the Why Choose Us section to sound like a real local owner wrote it — shorter sentences, more direct."
- "Add a sticky click-to-call bar at the bottom of the screen on mobile."
- "Replace the placeholder gallery images with [describe their actual job photos once you have them]."

## Pro tips

- **Reviews are your secret weapon.** Real reviews with real first names make the demo feel finished and personal. Always pull 3–5 from their Google listing before generating.
- **Build the site BEFORE you even call them.** (See business-plan-notes.md — demo-first is the whole strategy.) The generation costs you minutes; the demo closes the deal.
- **Photos:** at the appointment, ask them to text you 10 job photos from their phone. Swap them in before launch — real photos beat stock 10-to-1 in this market.
- If they serve multiple towns, get every town name into the copy — that's how they start ranking for "roofer Hatch NM" with zero competition.
